CogNivo is a dementia-support web application designed to make everyday care more manageable for both the person living with dementia and the family member looking after them. It brings cognitive activities, personal memories, care records, activity monitoring, safety support and everyday care management together in one platform. With a focus on accessibility, privacy, offline use and multilingual support, CogNivo aims to make digital dementia support more practical for families, including rural areas with limited internet connectivity.

1. Patient Mode: A familiar space to engage, relax and reconnect

Patient Mode provides a simple, structured and user-friendly environment where users can explore activities.Voice assistance offers additional ways to navigate the application and follow instructions, making interaction more accessible.

Cognitive Games: CogNivo offers a variety of games targeting memory, attention, visual processing speed, object and facial recognition, sequencing, recall and logical thinking. Cognitive stimulation is an established approach in dementia care, and research suggests that suitable activities may support cognitive engagement and aspects of wellbeing. 
Caregivers can customise available games and difficulty levels according to the user's abilities and needs, while recorded progress helps them review participation over time.

Move and Breathe: This section offers animated, guided movement and seated exercises suited to older adults. Users can choose session lengths, follow visual and spoken instructions at a comfortable pace, and pause or rest whenever necessary. Suitable movement may support mobility, flexibility and general wellbeing.

Feel and Reminisce: Users can explore meditation, mindfulness, ASMR sounds and ambient sensory experiences in a calming environment. They can choose atmospheres such as a forest, sunset or rainfall to create an experience that suits their preferences, offering a peaceful break within their daily routine.

Our Inner Circle: This feature brings together family trees, familiar faces, photographs, favourite places, music, life timelines and meaningful moments. It gives families a way to preserve and revisit parts of a person's life story, helping encourage recognition, conversation, emotional connection and a sense of familiarity.

2. Caregiver Mode: Making daily care easier to manage

Caregiver Mode is password-protected and provides a separate space for the person responsible for care. Beyond maintaining records, it brings together activity progress, trends, reminders, safety support and health information in an organised interface.

Daily Carelog: Caregivers can record observations about meals, water intake, personal hygiene, toileting, sleep, health and behaviour. Information is stored in structured categories, making it easier to enter, retrieve and review records instead of relying on scattered notes or memory alone.

Factors, Trends and Progress Analysis: Recorded information can be reviewed to identify patterns in daily routines, wellbeing and activity participation. Depending on the information collected, this includes memory recall, visual processing speed, routine consistency, task independence and reliance on assistance, alongside changes in sleep, food intake, mood and behaviour. Charts and visual summaries can make patterns easier to understand and help caregivers identify changes worth discussing with a healthcare professional. Cognitive-game progress provides additional insight into participation and performance. 

Medicine and Appointment Manager: Schedules and reminders help caregivers keep track of medicine timings, medical appointments and important daily routines, reducing the burden of managing multiple responsibilities.

Safety Alerts and Emergency Help: CogNivo includes an emergency-support area intended to make important assistance and emergency information easier to access. Safety alerts, where configured and supported, can help bring relevant events to the caregiver's attention. 

AI-Assisted Medical Report Summariser: A planned feature aims to simplify unfamiliar medical terminology and organise key information from medical reports into more understandable summaries.

Printable Care Reports: Relevant care records, trends and activity progress can be compiled into printable summaries for medical consultations. These reports help caregivers communicate observations more clearly without having to reconstruct every detail from memory, supporting more organised discussions with healthcare professionals.

3. Future Development: Personalised Care and Connected Support

Radar-Based Night and Wellbeing Monitoring: A planned addition is a 60 GHz millimetre-wave radar system integrated with CogNivo. It is intended to monitor the person during the night by detecting sleep patterns, breathing-related patterns, restlessness, bed exits and potential falls. The system could identify configured safety events and send alerts to caregivers, while relevant monitoring results would be displayed through the CogNivo application. This would help caregivers review night-time activity, notice changes that may require attention and respond to potential safety concerns more effectively.

Regional Language and Cultural Integration: CogNivo aims to move beyond basic translation by working with linguists, regional-language specialists and cultural experts to develop dementia-friendly content that feels familiar to the communities using it.Such collaboration would help ensure that translated content preserves its meaning and feels natural rather than simply converting words from one language to another.

Verified Healthcare Specialist Network: A planned specialist-access feature could help users and caregivers discover verified healthcare professionals, such as psychologists, neurologists, geriatricians and other relevant specialists, according to their location. This would make it easier to identify appropriate support nearby and connect families with qualified professionals. With suitable consent and privacy safeguards, relevant care information could be made available to authorised specialists to support more informed discussions and continuity of care.

These additions would extend CogNivo from a daily support platform towards a more connected dementia-care ecosystem, combining personalised activities, culturally relevant experiences, caregiver tools and access to professional support.
